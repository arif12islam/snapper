Snapper

Capture, annotate, record, share. A fast screenshot and screen recording app for your Mac.

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later.

Capture

Area, window, full screen or a scrolling page. Pull text out of any image.

Annotate

Arrows, shapes, text, a magnifier, blur and backgrounds, editable until you export.

Record and share

Record the screen or a GIF, trim it, pin shots, upload and copy a link.
