Snapper replaces the screenshot tool, the markup app and the screen recorder you switch between today.

Capture anything

An area, a window, the whole screen or a long scrolling page. Pull text out of any image too.

Annotate with intent

Arrows, shapes, numbered steps and text callouts. Everything stays editable until you export.

Magnify and redact

Zoom into the detail that matters with a loupe, and blur emails, names or keys in one drag.

Beautiful backgrounds

Drop any shot onto a wallpaper with padding, rounded corners and a soft shadow. Ready for a slide or a post.

Record and trim

Record the screen or a GIF, then trim it in a built-in editor before you send it.

Share a link

Upload in one click and the link is already on your clipboard. Pin shots to your screen for reference.

How it works

Three steps. No context switch.

Press a shortcut

Pick an area, a window or the full screen. Snapper lives in the menu bar and stays out of the way.

Mark it up

The editor opens instantly. Point, number, magnify, blur and frame it the way you would explain it.

Paste or share

Copy the image, save it, or upload and paste a link straight into Slack, email or a ticket.
