Capture an area, a window or the whole screen. Mark it up, record it, and share it as an image or a link, all from the menu bar.

What it does

Capture

An area, a window, the full screen or a scrolling page. Copy text out of any image.

Annotate

Arrows, shapes, numbered steps and text. Everything stays editable until you export.

Magnify and blur

Zoom into a detail with a loupe. Hide emails and names with a single drag.

Backgrounds

Place a shot on a background with padding and rounded corners, ready for slides.

Record

Record the screen or a GIF and trim it before you send it.

Share

Upload in one click and the link is copied. Pin shots on screen for reference.
