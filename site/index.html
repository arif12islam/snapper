Why a fish?

Snappers are reef fish with large, sharp eyes. Our icon is a close-up of one: the ring is its eye, the white curve is the edge of its gill.

A screenshot tool should notice the details too. That is the whole idea.

How it works

  1. Capture

    Press a shortcut and pick an area, a window, the whole screen or a long scrolling page.

  2. Mark it up

    Arrows, boxes, numbered steps, text, a magnifier and blur. All of it stays editable.

  3. Frame it

    Drop the shot on a coloured background with padding and rounded corners.

  4. Share it

    Copy the image, or upload it and paste the link straight into chat.
