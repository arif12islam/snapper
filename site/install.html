Install Snapper

Five steps, about two minutes. Snapper is in alpha and is not yet approved by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Drag Snapper to Applications

    Open the downloaded Snapper.dmg, then drag the Snapper icon onto the Applications folder.

  2. Open it, then close the warning

    Open Snapper from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Click Done. Do not choose Move to Trash.

  3. Approve it in System Settings

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security. Next to “Snapper was blocked”, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

    On macOS 14 you can instead right-click Snapper in Applications and choose Open.

  4. Click Open

    macOS asks once more. Click Open. Snapper appears in your menu bar and a short welcome guide starts.

  5. Allow screen recording

    The welcome guide asks for the Screen Recording permission. Without it every capture is blank. Turn Snapper on under Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen Snapper once.

Something went wrong?

Captures are blank: the Screen Recording permission is off or needs a restart of Snapper. Anything else: click the Snapper icon in the menu bar, choose Send Feedback, and tell us what happened. The diagnostics it adds help a lot.

Requirements

Any Mac with an M chip (M1 or newer) running macOS Sonoma (14) or newer. Not sure? Apple menu, About This Mac. Snapper checks for updates itself and installs them with one click.
